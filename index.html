About me

A senior frontend engineer who cares about how an app behaves under real-world data, not just how it looks in a demo.

Over the last five years I've worked almost exclusively on data-heavy, enterprise-grade products — logistics platforms, investment portals, financial document systems. These are apps where the hard part isn't the happy path; it's keeping the UI responsive while tens of thousands of rows stream in, where state management has to stay predictable across dozens of interconnected components, and where a single regression can break a regulated workflow.

That's the work I'm best at. I've built dynamic table engines, modular data-creation systems with validation and formatting rules, reusable filtering layers, and role-based permission flows. When the UI got slow, I profiled it and made it 3× faster. When the codebase got tangled, I led the refactoring. And because I'm comfortable across the stack, I've also written the NestJS / Node.js APIs and the CI/CD pipelines that feed and ship those features.

I work end-to-end, communicate clearly across teams, and lean on modern AI tooling (Claude, Codex, self-hosted models) to move faster without cutting corners on quality.
